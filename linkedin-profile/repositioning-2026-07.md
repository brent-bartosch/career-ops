# LinkedIn Repositioning — Brent Bartosch
### Goal: get sourced for Director / Head of RevOps · Head of Growth at Series A–B, targeting ~$250K TC

The whole strategy in one line: **stop leading with "GTM Engineer" (an IC keyword) and lead with "Revenue Operations / Growth leader who can also build the systems."** Same facts, senior framing. Recruiters search titles — so the target titles have to physically appear in your headline, your About, and your Open-to-Work settings, or their boolean searches never surface you.

---

## 1. Headline (highest-leverage single change)

Your headline is what recruiters search and what shows in every search result. It currently contains zero of the titles you want to be found for. Pick one of these — all are true to your record, all are keyword-loaded for recruiter search.

**Option A — RevOps lean (recommended for the $250K target)**
> Revenue Operations & Growth Leader | Enterprise SaaS seller ($800K+ closes) turned AI-native GTM systems architect | RevOps · Pipeline · CRM · Attribution · I build what most leaders outsource

**Option B — Growth lean**
> Head of Growth / RevOps | I set the revenue strategy and build the infrastructure that runs it | 7 yrs enterprise SaaS revenue + AI-native GTM engineering (Python/SQL/Claude)

**Option C — Punchy hybrid**
> Revenue leader who builds the machine | RevOps & GTM strategy + AI-native systems | $800K enterprise closer → GTM infrastructure architect | Director / Head of RevOps · Growth

**Why these work:** each front-loads a *leadership* identity, proves it with an enterprise revenue number, and treats the engineering as the *edge* rather than the job. Each also literally contains "RevOps," "Revenue Operations," "Head of Growth," or "Director" so you appear in those searches.

You're the founder — you have legitimate latitude, so use a title that signals seniority, not "Engineer."

## 5. Experience bullets — reframe pattern

Rewrite each bullet to lead with **decision + business outcome**, then let the mechanism be the proof. The plumbing detail (state machines, DLQs, test counts) moves to the *end* of the bullet or comes out entirely — it's catnip for an engineering manager and noise for a CRO hiring a revenue leader.

- ❌ "Built an 8-stage trial-conversion engine with independent AI quality gates and deterministic routing."
- ✅ "Cut client CAC ~90% by rebuilding the trial-to-revenue motion end to end — signup to sales-ready in under 90 seconds — and architected the system that runs it."

- ❌ "Built cross-channel attribution across Google Ads, GA4 & Search Console."
- ✅ "Surfaced $81K/year in misdirected ad spend and showed organic outperforming paid 2.5x, then rebuilt the campaign architecture — as the sole growth/analytics hire in a FedRAMP-authorized environment."

Lead every bullet you can with a dollar figure, a pipeline figure, or a CAC/LTV/forecast figure. Those are the units directors are measured in.
