# Profile — Brent Bartosch

## Positioning
GTM Systems Architect. I build bespoke go-to-market infrastructure — lead intelligence, pipeline automation, trial conversion engines, outbound systems — wired directly to APIs, with AI embedded as an engineering component.

## What I Build
- **Trial Conversion Engines** — 8-stage pipelines: intake, enrichment, behavioral signal extraction, ICP scoring, confidence-tiered routing, dossier generation, personalized email drafting, independent quality gate. ~10,300 LOC, 7 ICP cohorts, built for a $30M B2B SaaS platform.
- **Lead Intelligence Layers** — Firmographic + technographic enrichment, multi-source data fusion, scoring against defined ICPs.
- **Outbound Intelligence Systems** — Automated sequencing with personalization derived from enrichment data, not merge-field templates.
- **Content Engines** — Systematic content generation and distribution pipelines.
- **CRM Orchestration** — Direct API integration with Salesforce, HubSpot. No middleware, no iPaaS layer.
- **Spend Attribution** — Marketing spend tracking with attribution modeling.

## Approach
- API-direct: systems wire to CRM, enrichment, and analytics APIs directly. No Zapier, no Make, no middleware.
- AI as engineering component: embedded where it earns its place, with deterministic scaffolding, confidence scoring, typed output validation, independent quality gates.
- Pre-mortem methodology: failure modes identified before implementation.
- Speculative builds: I diagnose problems worth engineering against and build full systems as proof of capability.
