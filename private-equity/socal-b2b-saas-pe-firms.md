## The filter: what "like K1" actually means

From the K1 research, the traits that make K1 the right home for this model:

1. **B2B software / SaaS focus** (software-only or software-heavy)
2. **Lower-mid-market entry** (~$5–50M ARR, scaling toward $100–200M+)
3. **Buy-and-build / roll-up** — serial tuck-in acquisitions (this is the *repeatable GTM problem* the pitch solves)
4. **A dedicated operating / value-creation arm** — the counterparty that buys a "function," not a headcount (K1's analog: K1 Operations LLC)
5. **Portfolio scale** — dozens of companies so a redeployable GTM-AI function earns its keep
6. **Local office** — for the "come into the office when you need me" advantage

## The "come into the office" angle — how to play it

The local advantage isn't uniformly distributed, and it's worth being precise about it:

- **At the firm level (strong):** Marlin (Hermosa Beach), Clearlake (Santa Monica), Platinum (Beverly Hills), Kayne (Century City), JMI (San Diego) all have a physical office in your radius. The pitch — *"someone who can do the job, and walk in when you need them"* — is credible at the **HQ** level for all five.
- **At the person level (mixed):** the operating/value-creation people themselves are often distributed (London, Dallas, SLC, Singapore, Baltimore, NY). The only **confirmed-LA operating partner** in this sweep is **Ben Garrick (Clearlake)**. Marlin's Scott Lewis and Sarah Hollyhead are HQ-anchored (Hermosa Beach) and very likely local.
- **The honest framing:** for a PE operating partner, "local" matters less than "available at the portco." The office card is a tiebreaker and a trust signal, not the whole pitch. Lead with the K1-proven substance (buy-and-build GTM integration, named products, metric deltas); use "and I'm 20 minutes from your office" as the closer.

## Data provenance & caveats

**How this was built:**
- **Firm discovery + facts** — Serper (Google) web search, cross-referenced against firm sites and LinkedIn/Wikipedia/CapitalAUM.
- **People + emails/LinkedIn** — Apollo People API (the current `mixed_people/api_search` + `people/match` endpoints; the older `mixed_people/search` is deprecated and was bypassed).
- **Bright Data** — not used for *discovery* here, because the wired-up datasets are **profile + activity** enrichment (LinkedIn person detail + recent posts), not company/people *search*. It's the right tool for the *next* step: once you shortlist individuals, pull their recent LinkedIn activity to craft a personalized hook (same flow as `outbound/` Stage 4).

**Caveats (flag before you send anything):**
- AUM figures are **approximate** and move with each fundraise (Clearlake closed $14.8B in June 2026; Platinum/Marlin figures are the most recently public). Verify against the firm's latest ADV/announcement before quoting in an email.
- Apollo emails/LinkedIn are **machine-sourced** — treat as "verify before outreach," especially the Kayne one (`pagrawal@kaynecapital.com` vs. the `kayneanderson.com` corporate domain).
- "Local vs. remote" on named contacts is from Apollo's location field and may be stale. Confirm current office/remote status before leaning on the local angle.
- Gores' AUM is genuinely opaque (the RIA filing captures only a small registered slice). Treat its scale claim cautiously.

**Tooling:** `private-equity/research.mjs` (in this folder) is the reusable helper — `serper`, `apollo`, `apollo-match`, `match-list`, `fetch`. Keys come from the project `.env` (`SERPER_API_KEY`, `APOLLO_API_KEY`). Re-run any query with `node private-equity/research.mjs <cmd> <args>`.
