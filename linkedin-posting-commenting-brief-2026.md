# LinkedIn Content & Commenting Brief — GTM Engineer (2026)

*Handoff brief for the career-ops working session on posting & commenting.*
*Sourced from a verified deep-research pass: 20 sources, 25 claims adversarially checked, 15 confirmed / 10 refuted. Primary sources: LinkedIn Engineering blog, LinkedIn Help (a414283), Richard van der Blom, AuthoredUp, Buffer/Socialinsider datasets. Compiled 2026-06-01.*

---

## ⚠️ Read this first — posting ≠ recruiter discoverability

**Confirmed (3-0):** LinkedIn **Recruiter** discovery runs on *profile* signals, not feed activity. The "Active Talent" spotlight keys on profile updates, recent login, resume upload, and Open-to-Work status — **not on whether you post.** So:

- **Profile optimization + Open to Work** = the machine that makes you *rank in recruiter search.*
- **Posting + commenting** = a *different* machine that builds audience, authority, and **inbound DMs** (recruiters, founders, clients who find you through the feed, not search).

Both matter, but don't expect posting to move your recruiter-search ranking. It moves inbound.

> **SSI tie-in:** Brent's current SSI is 51/100 (top 10% in industry), but "Engage with insights" is only 1.77/25 — the single biggest gap, and exactly the muscle this brief is about. It will climb the moment commenting resumes.

## 1. Feed algorithm mechanics (confirmed)

- **Three core ranking signals** (3-0): early engagement velocity, dwell time, and content relevance/quality.
- **Golden hour is real** (3-0): your post goes to a ~10% trial pool first; strong engagement in the first **30–90 min** gates whether reach expands. Post when your audience is live and engage immediately.
- **Comments > likes** (3-0): comments are weighted meaningfully more than likes in distribution. ⚠️ The viral "comments count 2x/15x more" *specific multipliers* were **refuted** — direction is true, the numbers are made up.
- **Dwell time is a confirmed signal** (3-0) — but the specific "61s = 15.6% engagement, outweighs likes" stat was **refuted**. Write posts people *stop and read*, don't chase a number.
- **Saves & shares** are the strongest positive engagement signals in the first hour (3-0).
- **Post lifespan:** 2–3 weeks — good evergreen content keeps resurfacing.

## 2. Format ranking (confirmed)

- **Carousels / PDF documents → highest *engagement*** (3-0).
- **Native video → highest *reach*** (3-0).
- **Text-only → weakest** of the major formats.
- **Polls → reach trap**, avoid.
- **External links:** they *do* suppress reach, but ~**19–27%** (van der Blom ~18.8%), **not** the "50–70%" claim — that and the **"put the link in the first comment" hack were both refuted (0-3).** Don't make link-drops your main format; native content first.

## 3. Commenting as a growth lever

- **Supported in principle, but the quantified tactics are bunk.** Refuted (0-3): "comment 10–15x/day on big accounts → 50–100 profile visits/day → 10–20 followers/week," the "35% first-hour visibility boost," "comments under 10 words ignored / NLP-scored," and "indirect comments = 2.4x reach."
- **What's actually true:** thoughtful comments on relevant posts put you in front of that author's audience and drive profile views → some convert to follows/inbound. It works; the specific yield numbers floating around are fabricated.
- **Practical play:** comment early (golden hour) on posts from people whose audience overlaps your ICP, add a genuine take or build-detail (not "great post"), and treat it as *consistent presence*, not a daily quota chasing a refuted stat.
- **Open question (unverified):** the real conversion rate from comment-driven profile views → recruiter/client inbound. Treat as "directionally positive, unmeasured."

## 4. Cadence (confirmed, with a caveat)

- **3–5 posts/week**, ~**one per 24h**. A second same-day post **cannibalizes** both (engagement drops past ~5/week).
- ⚠️ **Refuted (0-3):** "more frequency = more reach with no penalty across all account sizes." There *is* a ceiling. The specific "best times 7am–4pm weekdays" was also weak (1-2) — test your own audience.

## 5. Content pillars for a technical/GTM builder

Confirmed as the formats that work for practitioners building authority:

- **Build-in-public** — what you're shipping at Smoothed, in progress
- **System teardowns / breakdowns** — how a real pipeline works, architecture
- **Tool comparisons** — Clay vs. X, n8n workflows, etc.
- **Case studies** — problem → system → outcome, with numbers
- **Frameworks** — repeatable mental models
- **Hot takes** — contrarian, opportunity-framed (fits the Smoothed voice)

These map directly to the "builder" GTM-Engineer identity and Brent's existing build work — strongest raw material available.

## Myths to ignore (all refuted under verification)

Comments worth 2x/15x likes · dwell = 15.6% magic number · links cut reach 50–70% · "link in first comment" hack · 10–15 comments/day → 50–100 visits · 35% first-hour boost · indirect comments 2.4x · format-doesn't-matter · more posting always = more reach.
